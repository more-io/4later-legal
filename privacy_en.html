2. Information on the Use of the App

2.1 Sign-in with Apple or Google Account: Using the App requires signing in with an Apple account or a Google account. Name and email address are stored (with Apple Sign-In optionally via Private Relay, without a plaintext email address). There is no email/password sign-in; 4later does not process any passwords.

2.2 Legacy accounts without sign-in: During an earlier testing phase, accounts could be created without signing in (anonymously). Such legacy accounts remain usable and can be linked to an Apple or Google account at any time (all data is preserved). New anonymous accounts have not been created since July 30, 2026.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data collected through the use of the App is processed and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure within the European Union:

Firestore database: EU multi-region (eur3, Belgium and Netherlands)

Cloud Storage (files, attachments): EU multi-region

Cloud Functions (server-side processing): europe-west1 (Belgium)

Google Gemini (AI analysis): EU multi-region

Screenshot service (our own infrastructure): europe-west1 (Belgium)

Personal Data: When signing in via Apple or Google, name and email address are stored and used for synchronization across multiple devices. For anonymous legacy accounts (Section 2.2), no names or email addresses are collected.

All data transmissions between the App, Google services, and our own services are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 (Google-managed keys).

Exceptions with third-country transfer: Push notifications are delivered via Apple Push Notification Service (APNS, USA) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Apple processes this data under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Support requests via Freshdesk are processed in the USA and India (see Section 7).

4. Automatically Collected Data

Our App uses the following Google Firebase services to provide core functionality:

Firebase Authentication: User account management (UID, optional email/name)

Cloud Firestore: Storage of user content (Topics, Items, Filters, Reminders)

Cloud Storage: Storage of uploaded files (PDFs, images, documents)

Cloud Functions: Server-side business logic (e.g. email import, thumbnail generation)

Firebase Cloud Messaging: Delivery of push notifications (device tokens)

Firebase App Check: Protection of server APIs against unauthorized access (device attestation tokens)

Device and Installation Data: When the App launches and when it returns to the foreground, the following data is transmitted for each device you use to our servers (Firebase, europe-west1) and stored under your user ID: a random device-internal installation identifier (Firebase Installations ID), app version, operating system version, hardware model, platform (iPhone/iPad/Mac), language, timezone, the push notification token, the distribution channel of the App (App Store or TestFlight), and the country of your App Store account (storefront, e.g. "Germany" – this is NOT location tracking, but the country setting of your Apple account). Purpose: correct localization of reminders and maintenance jobs to your timezone (performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), delivery of push notifications (performance of contract), diagnosis of errors, and usage-based improvement of the App (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: until account deletion; devices inactive for more than 12 months are automatically removed.

Subscription Status: Your current subscription tier (e.g. "Premium Monthly") and the current renewal date are stored in your account to provide your feature scope across all your devices.

Entry Source per Item: For each stored item, we record how the item was created (e.g. in-app entry, share extension, action extension, email import, clipboard paste). Purpose: improvement of input pathways.

Internal Diagnostic Statistics: For diagnosis and maintenance, we store statistics about internal cron jobs (number of processed entries, duration, any errors) for up to 12 months, as well as daily anonymized usage aggregates (daily statistics) indefinitely.

The App does NOT use analytics or tracking services. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising IDs, or browser fingerprinting. No location data, contact data, or health data is collected.

5. Storage Duration and Deletion

Your personal data will be stored for as long as is necessary for the provision of our services. You have the option to delete your user account and all associated data at any time via the App settings.

Content moved to the bin is automatically deleted after a user-configurable period (from immediate to 1 year).

If you uninstall the App without first deleting your user account, your data will remain stored in our cloud database (Google Firestore and Cloud Storage).

6. Specific Data Protection Features of 4later

6.1 Data Transmission and Security:

All data transmissions between the App and our server services are TLS-encrypted.

The App does not store sensitive personal information locally outside the user's device sandbox.

6.2 Anonymous Legacy Accounts:

Users who still use the App anonymously from the earlier testing phase (Section 2.2) can link their data to an Apple or Google account at any time; all content is preserved.

6.3 Data Security:

4later UG (haftungsbeschränkt) takes all necessary technical and organizational measures to ensure data security. Documentation of the technical and organizational measures (TOM) pursuant to Art. 32 GDPR is available upon request.

6.4 Spotlight Indexing (iOS System Search):

If you have enabled "Findable in Spotlight" in the App settings (default: enabled), 4later makes the following content of your entries available to the local iOS Spotlight search: title, notes, AI summaries, and the name of the parent topic. If an attachment has a cached thumbnail image, this is also provided. This data is held exclusively in the local system search index on your device and is not transmitted to either 4later or Apple. You can disable the feature at any time in the 4later settings or in iOS Settings – Siri \& Search – 4later.

6.5 On-Device Processing of Voice Memos:

Transcription of voice recordings (audio items) is performed exclusively on your device (Apple SpeechAnalyzer). The audio content of voice-memo items is not transmitted to our servers or to Google Gemini for this purpose. The audio track of video attachments is an exception and is processed as part of AI video analysis (see Section 7).

6.6 Shared Topics (Topic Sharing):

Topics can be shared with other 4later users. The following applies:

- Visibility of profile data: When sharing or joining, your profile name, your profile picture (if set), and your role (owner, editor, or viewer) become visible to all members of the topic – live: if you change your name or picture, the other members immediately see the current version. There is no setting to hide your name or picture from members – anyone participating in shared topics always shows the other participants their current profile name and profile picture. A profile name is required for sharing and joining. Members can also see each member's join date. Technical note: your profile record (name and picture) can be retrieved by signed-in 4later users who know your user ID – in practice, these are your fellow members in shared topics; browsing or listing all profiles is technically prevented.

- Invitation and join request: Joining happens via an invitation link and a join request that the owner approves or declines. Your profile name is transmitted to the owner together with the request. When the owner shares the invitation link, the link preview shows every recipient of the link the owner's profile name, the topic title, and the number of entries.

- Shared content: Items, attachments, titles, notes, and AI summaries of a shared topic are visible to all members according to their role. Server-side AI analysis (Section 7) also processes content contributed by other members.

- Read status: For shared topics, your personal read status per item is stored in your own user area. The owner of a shared topic can see whether individual members have read an item. The app points this out before you join. Members cannot see each other's read status.

- Leaving and removal: When you leave a shared topic or are removed by the owner, your access to the topic ends. Your personal read marks remain in your user area (and apply again if you rejoin); they are deleted when your account is deleted.

- Deletion of shared topics: If the owner deletes or archives a shared topic, or deletes their account, sharing ends; the topic and its contents are deleted according to the rules in Section 5 and are no longer accessible to any member.

- Reporting content: Members of a shared topic can report an item they consider objectionable (reasons: objectionable content, harassment, other, each with optional free text). We then store your user identifier, the topic owner's user identifier, the identifiers of the topic and the item, the selected reason, your free text, and the time and processing status — on our servers (Firebase, europe-west1). The reported person does not learn who filed the report. Reports are deliberately NOT subject to an automatic deletion period, so that a case remains traceable during and after handling; they are deleted once they are no longer required for handling and any evidentiary purposes. For handling, a notification is posted to an internal channel of the 4later team (Slack, see Section 7). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing abuse and in meeting platform requirements for user-generated content). 7. Integration of Third-Party Services and APIs

Our App integrates various services to provide enhanced functionality:

Slack (Salesforce): When an abuse report is filed (Section 6.6), our server posts a notification to an internal, non-public channel of the 4later team on Slack. That notification contains no names, no content and no user identifiers — only the type of report and an internal incident number that can be linked to the case solely within our internal processing system. Profile names, user identifiers, the topic title and the report's free text remain exclusively on our servers (Firebase, europe-west1). Slack is a service of Salesforce, Inc.; processing outside the EU cannot be ruled out; it is safeguarded by the Data Processing Addendum concluded with Salesforce, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. No other feature of the App transmits data to Slack.

YouTube Data API (Google): Enables retrieval of public metadata for YouTube videos (title, description, thumbnails). Only the public video ID is transmitted to the YouTube API — no personal data. The request is made server-side from our Cloud Function in europe-west1.

Mailgun (Sinch): The App offers an optional email import feature where emails can be forwarded to a personal 4later address. Incoming email processing is handled by Mailgun (Sinch Email) in the EU region Frankfurt. Email content and attachments are stored by Mailgun for a maximum of 7 days and then automatically deleted. If large attachments are sent by Apple Mail as an iCloud link (Mail Drop), our server retrieves these attachments from Apple's iCloud servers for storage. A rate limit of 50 emails per day per user applies for abuse protection. Mailgun acts as a data processor in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Certifications: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001. Data Protection Officer: DPO@sinch.com.

Google Gemini: The App uses the Google Gemini API (part of the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, formerly Vertex AI) to automatically analyze attachment content (e.g. PDFs, images, documents, and videos). For video attachments, both the visual content and the video's audio track are analyzed to generate a title and description. Content is sent to the Google Gemini API to generate automatic summaries and descriptions. For saved web links, the App additionally captures a snapshot of the page content on your device at save time and uploads it to your entry; if the page is later unreachable for our server (e.g. requires a login), the summary is generated from this snapshot. The App also uses Google Gemini for automatic topic filing: a description of each topic is derived from its contents, each entry is assessed as to whether it fits its topic, and given a clear recommendation the entry is automatically filed into the matching topic (can be disabled in the settings). For this purpose, the titles and notes of your entries, AI summaries, and the titles and descriptions of your topics are processed. Processing takes place in the EU multi-region. In accordance with Google Cloud Terms of Service, Google does not use this data to train AI models. A zero data retention policy applies, meaning customer data is not permanently stored after processing. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing intelligent app features).

AI Assistant (Chat): The App offers an optional AI assistant that lets you ask questions about your saved items in natural language. To answer your question and find matching items, your chat message, the prior conversation, and relevant content of your items (e.g. titles, notes, AI summaries, and selected attachment content) are sent to the Google Gemini API (EU multi-region). The same conditions apply as for attachment analysis (no use for model training, zero data retention). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Only anonymous, non-personal usage counters are collected for improvement and abuse prevention – queries and responses are not stored.

Connecting External AI Assistants (MCP interface): You can connect your own AI assistant (e.g. Claude by Anthropic or ChatGPT by OpenAI) to your 4later data via a secure interface (MCP, OAuth 2.1). The connection is established exclusively by you: during the connection flow, you sign in with your Apple or Google account and explicitly authorize the access. The connected assistant can then search and read your entries (topics, titles, notes, AI summaries, attachments via short-lived download links, and, on request, your AI chat history); write access is currently not available. Important: content retrieved by your AI assistant is transmitted to its provider and processed there under that provider's own privacy policy – depending on the provider, also outside the EU (e.g. USA). 4later has no influence on this processing; please consult the privacy policy of your AI provider. You can disconnect at any time with your AI provider; if unused, the connection expires by itself after 30 days at the latest (access tokens already expire after 60 minutes).
