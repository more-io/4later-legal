2. Sign-in

2.1 The App requires sign-in with an Apple or Google account. We store your name and email address (with Apple, if you wish, only an address forwarded by Apple, Private Relay) for your account and for synchronization across your devices (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not process passwords. Without sign-in data the App cannot be used; all further information is voluntary.

2.2 Existing anonymous accounts from an earlier test phase remain usable and can be linked to an Apple or Google account at any time without losing content. For them we collect neither a name nor an email address. They cannot take out a subscription, share a topic or request an export. No new anonymous accounts have been created since July 30, 2026.

3. Storage Location and Security

We store and process your data in the EU with Google Cloud and Google Firebase (the Google platform 4later runs on):

- the database (Firestore) in the EU multi-region eur3 (Belgium and the Netherlands);

- the file storage for attachments (Cloud Storage) in the EU multi-region;

- the programs on our servers (Cloud Functions) and our own screenshot service in europe-west1 (Belgium);

- the AI analysis (Google Gemini) in the EU multi-region, under heavy load in europe-west3 (Frankfurt) instead.

Transfers are encrypted with TLS, stored data with AES-256 (keys managed by Google). We provide our technical and organizational measures under Art. 32 GDPR on request. When data may leave the EU (push notifications, Apple services, YouTube requests, support, Dropbox, Slack and AI assistants of other providers that you connect) is stated in section 7.

4. Automatically Collected Data

Device and installation data: At launch and when it comes to the foreground, the App stores for each of your devices, under your user ID on our servers:

- a random installation identifier (Firebase Installations ID), App and operating system version, device model, platform, language and time zone;

- the push token (your device's delivery address for push notifications) and the permission status for push notifications and calendar;

- the time of first and last use, the distribution channel (App Store or TestFlight, Apple's test program) and the country of your App Store account (not your location);

- daily counters without content: usage time and sessions of the App and its extensions, widgets you have set up, taps on widgets and Control Center buttons, the App's rating prompts and the "connection problem" screen.

Purpose: reminders and maintenance runs in your time zone and the delivery of push notifications (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), error diagnosis and improvement of the App (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: until the account is deleted, the daily counters 14 days each; the App removes the push token when you sign out.

You can see your devices in the device list under Settings > User account > Connected devices and remove other devices there. If a device runs an older App version without a device identifier and has not been opened for 90 days, the App removes its device entry at its next launch on one of your devices; if the device is used again, it reappears.

So that a device does not appear several times in this list after the App is reinstalled, the App stores a random device key in the device's keychain (Keychain, the protected storage of iOS). It survives deletion of the App and never leaves the device. Only a device identifier derived from it (hash), different for every account, is sent to our servers. With it, the App merges older entries of the same device in your account into one entry (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The App stores the installation identifier and the device key on your device because the features you use strictly need them (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

Subscription: We store your plan, the end of the paid period, the product identifier, Apple's transaction identifier and a purchase identifier derived from your user ID. If several 4later accounts use the subscription of one Apple ID, we also store which accounts last used it and when, to enforce the number allowed per plan. With Apple's Family Sharing we do not learn who belongs to it (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Input source: For each entry we record how it was created (e.g. App, share or action extension, email or folder import, clipboard, a connected external AI assistant under section 7.2), to improve the input paths (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Diagnostic statistics: About internal scheduled maintenance runs we store statistics (number of entries processed, duration, errors) for up to 12 months, anonymized daily statistics permanently (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services, advertising IDs or fingerprinting. We do not collect your location, contacts from your address book or health data.

5. Storage Duration and Deletion

We store your content and account data as long as you have your account. If you delete your account in the App settings, we delete them. Only abuse reports, as long as they are needed (6.3), Mailgun delivery reports for up to 180 days (7.3) and a Dropbox access key until it is revoked (6.7) remain longer. In our database backups, deleted data remains for up to 98 days (point-in-time recovery 7 days), in the database access logs 30 days. Deleting only the App deletes no data. The App empties the trash after a period of your choice (1 day, 7 days, 30 days, 6 months, 1 year or never).

Unused accounts: If an account is not used for 12 months, we delete it with all content, including its shared topics together with the entries of other members. Access through a connected AI assistant (connector), reading or writing, also counts as use. We announce this by email to the account's address about 30, 14 and 3 days in advance (for a hidden Apple address, to the address forwarded by Apple); signing in to the App once is enough to prevent it. Accounts without an email address, such as existing anonymous accounts, are not deleted this way. Accounts with an active subscription are exempt; after it expires, the 12 months begin at the earliest on the expiry date. We keep a copy for 1 year in non-public storage in the EU (Google Cloud Storage) to restore the account on request; after that we delete it. Legal basis: legitimate interest in not storing data longer than necessary (Art. 6(1)(f) in conjunction with Art. 5(1)(e) GDPR).

6. Individual Features

6.1 Spotlight: If "Findable in Spotlight" is on (default), the App hands title, notes, AI summaries (for voice memos the transcript), file names, thumbnails and topic names of your entries to the system search on your device. This also applies to entries from shared topics. 4later does not read this index; Apple may use it for Siri and Apple Intelligence (6.4). Turning it off in the 4later settings or in iOS under Settings > Apps > 4later clears the index. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6.2 Voice memos, spoken questions and scanned documents: The App converts voice recordings to text only on your device (Apple SpeechAnalyzer). We store the recording like any attachment, it never goes to Google Gemini; we process the transcript like other text (section 7.1). For videos, Gemini also analyzes the audio track. A spoken question to "Ask 4later" is also converted on the device; the audio is neither stored nor transmitted, only the text. The App recognizes the text of scanned pages only on your device (text recognition, Apple Vision); it stores the result as an invisible text layer in the PDF, which we process like any attachment (section 7.1).

6.3 Shared topics (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for abuse reports Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):

- Members see your profile name (required for sharing and joining), your profile picture, your role (owner, editor, contributor or viewer) and the date you joined, always current and with no option to hide them. Your profile can only be retrieved by signed-in users who know your user ID.

- You join through an invitation link and a request that the owner receives with your profile name and accepts or declines; open requests expire after 14 days. We log invitations, joins and refusals for 90 days. Anyone who opens the link in the App while signed in sees the owner's profile name, the topic title and the number of entries beforehand; the link's web page shows none of this.

- All members see the content according to their role; the AI analysis also processes contributions of other members.

- The owner sees which members have read an entry (the App points this out before you join), members do not see this about each other. We store your read status, flags and done marks for shared entries for you only, until you delete your account; nobody else sees your flags.

- Leaving or being removed ends your access. Entries you placed in someone else's topic stay there, even if you leave it or delete your account. If the owner archives the topic, all members' access ends and the content stays with the owner; if the owner deletes the topic or their account, the content is deleted under section 5.

- You can report up to 20 entries per day. We store abuse reports about entries with reason, free text, time, processing status and the identifiers of you, the owner, the topic and the entry; the reported person does not learn who reported. We delete them as soon as they are no longer needed for handling and evidence. Legal basis: legitimate interest in preventing abuse and in the platform requirements for user-generated content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For the internal notice via Slack see section 7.3.

6.4 Siri and Shortcuts: Through Shortcuts and Siri (from iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence) you can create, change, move, open, search and count entries, record voice memos and ask 4later questions. Apple processes your voice input under its own privacy notices (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/ and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/intelligence-engine/); 4later receives only the recognized text. On request, 4later gives Siri matching entries (title, notes, topic, flag, reminder, done status, links), including from shared topics, and the entry shown. Entries you create or change through Siri we process as in the App (input source "shortcut"); the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Questions to 4later we process like questions to the AI assistant, under its legal basis (section 7.1). Turn off: in iOS under Settings > Apps > 4later.

6.5 Widgets and Control Center: You set up both yourself in iOS. Widgets show due, unread or the newest entries of a topic (title, topic, reminder, count), including from shared topics. For this the data is kept in a file in a protected area of your device and does not leave it; after you sign out or when the account is locked, the App removes the titles from it. On the Lock Screen, widgets may be visible even while the device is locked, depending on your iOS settings. The Control Center buttons open the App, the "Voice Memo" button directly with a recording; they show no content. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for the usage statistics see section 4.

6.6 QR codes: The App recognizes QR codes in images and PDFs on your device and stores the links and payment codes it finds with the attachment. On your tap it hands a payment code to your banking app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6.7 Folder import (iCloud Drive and Dropbox): PDFs and images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC) that you put into an import folder become entries in a topic of your choice. You turn both paths on and off in the settings under "Import".

- iCloud Drive: The App reads the folder "4later/Import" on your device while it is open, asks our server with a file's hash and size whether it is new, and uploads it; 4later gets no access to your iCloud account. A file next to the folder with a hash of your user ID binds it to one 4later account.

- Dropbox: You allow the app "4later Import" access only to the folder "Apps/4later Import": reading, changing and deleting files there. 4later only deletes imported files there and changes nothing else. When Dropbox reports a change to our server (with the identifier of your Dropbox account), the server downloads the new files, even while your device is off. We store the access key (encrypted), the identifier of your Dropbox account, a position marker (how far the folder has been read), the folder and the times and status of the connection, not the name or email address of your Dropbox account.

- If "Delete file after import" is on (default), a file is only deleted once it is completely stored (in iCloud Drive into "Recently Deleted", recoverable there for 30 days, in Dropbox under its rules).

- Against duplicate imports we keep, per account, a register with the SHA-256 hash of each file, source, size, time, import month and the resulting entry with its topic, for Dropbox also Dropbox's check value; file names and content are not in it. It remains even if you delete an entry.

- If you disconnect Dropbox or delete your account, we delete the connection data, with the account also the register, and revoke the access key at Dropbox. Until the revocation succeeds, for at most 30 days, we keep the key encrypted for that purpose only. You can also remove the access in Dropbox under "Connected apps".

Imported files are processed like any attachment. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. AI Features, Connector and Recipients

7.1 AI features (Google Gemini):

- Analysis: Our server has attachments (PDFs, images, documents, videos including the audio track) and web links summarized. When you save a web link, the App always uploads a snapshot of the page; the summary is made from it if our server cannot fetch the page. With "Use as preview" in the built-in browser the App also uploads image and text of a page you are signed in to (visible to all members in shared topics).

- Topic assignment: Topic descriptions are derived from the titles, notes and AI summaries of the entries; if an entry clearly fits another topic, the App moves it there. There is no automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR; automatic sorting can be turned off.

- AI assistant (chat with a large language model, LLM): your message, the conversation history and matching content of your entries. We store the history, including Siri questions, and for each answer which of your entries and attachments it read (as identifiers only), in your account until you delete conversations or the account. For the monthly quota we count your requests, otherwise we only collect anonymous counters.

- Semantic search: From the title, notes and AI summary of an entry, the titles and summaries of up to five attachments and the topic title we compute an embedding (a sequence of numbers for the meaning, from which the text cannot be recovered). We store it with the topic and delete it with the entry, the topic or the account.

Google processes this in the EU, does not use the data for training and does not store it permanently after processing (Zero Data Retention). Legal basis: legitimate interest in intelligent features and an accurate search (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7.2 External AI assistants (connector): You can connect your own AI assistant, such as Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI), to 4later via MCP (Model Context Protocol, an open standard for AI assistants' data access). Only you grant access, after signing in with Apple or Google, via OAuth 2.1 without handing over a password. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the connection you set up).

- The assistant can search and read your entries: topics, titles, notes, AI summaries, attachments through short-lived download links and, on request, your chat histories. Only if you choose "Write" when connecting can it, on your behalf, also do largely what you can do in the App with your entries, attachments and topics, except sharing, re-recognizing QR codes and a general undo. This includes permanently deleting entries that are already in the trash or the archive, and your own, unshared topics with all their entries and attachments. Anything permanently deleted cannot be restored. In shared topics the rights of your role apply, and the members see what the assistant creates there. A daily maximum limits the changes. A connection you granted for reading only can write only after you connect again.

- We instruct the assistant to make changes only on your explicit instruction; whether it asks first depends on its settings. We log every change for 30 days in your account: time, service, kind and result, topic and entry, a short title and the changed fields before and after, title and note only as a hash. Your data export contains the log without identifiers and hashes.

- What the assistant retrieves is processed by its provider under its own terms, depending on the provider outside the EU (e.g. USA); we have no influence on this.

- For each connection we store the identifier and name of the connected application, the access level, the times of connecting and of the last renewal and the access key only as a hash. You can disconnect at the provider or under Settings > User account > Connected connectors; an access token already issued remains valid for up to 60 minutes after disconnecting. We delete the connection data when you disconnect and with your account. Every use renews the connection; unused, it expires after 30 days, and we then delete its data, usually the following day. We also store for your account the day on which you last used 4later through a connector (updated at most once a day); it counts as use for the deletion of unused accounts (section 5) and is deleted with your account.

- When an application is newly connected, we inform you of its name and internet address, the access level and the time, to protect your account against access you did not allow: by email to your account address, which cannot be turned off, and by push notification according to your notification setting. We do not collect IP addresses or location for this. If the same application is connected with the same access level several times within 10 minutes, you receive only one notice (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

11. Glossary

- AI assistant: A program that understands and produces language. The App's AI assistant is the chat (section 7.1); a connected external assistant is such a program of another provider (section 7.2).

- Adequacy decision: A decision of the EU Commission that a country outside the EU protects data adequately; transfers there are then allowed without further contracts.

- App Check: A protection by Google Firebase that checks that a request really comes from the 4later App.

- Download link, short-lived: A link to one of your files that becomes invalid after a few minutes.

- EU multi-region: Data is stored mirrored at several locations within the EU and does not leave the EU.

- EU-US Data Privacy Framework: An arrangement between the EU and the USA; US companies certified under it are considered sufficiently safe for personal data.

- Hash (SHA-256): A check value computed from content, from which the content cannot be recovered; it serves for recognition.

- OAuth 2.1: A standard with which you give a program access to your data without revealing your password to it; the access can be revoked at any time.

- Push notification: A message from the App on your device, delivered via Apple or Google, even when the App is not open.

- Standard contractual clauses: Contract texts set by the EU Commission with which a recipient outside the EU commits to equivalent data protection.

- Text recognition (OCR, optical character recognition): a method that reads the text in an image, such as a scanned page, as characters so that it can be searched and copied.

- TLS, AES-256: TLS encrypts data on the way between App and server, AES-256 encrypts it while stored.

- Token (access token): A time-limited digital key with which a program accesses data within an authorization.

- Widget: A small window of the App on the Home Screen or Lock Screen.
